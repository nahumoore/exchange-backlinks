## What is Featured.com?

[Featured.com](https://www.featured.com/) (formerly Terkel) is a two-sided marketplace: publishers post questions, and experts answer them for a chance to be quoted — and linked — in the resulting article. It offers a free tier plus paid plans (Premium around $99/month) that add an AI co-pilot to surface and auto-respond to opportunities across platforms. In April 2025, Featured.com acquired the HARO brand from Cision and relaunched it as a free email digest after Cision's paid "Connectively" rebrand was rejected by users — so Featured now effectively runs both formats.

## Where Featured.com still wins

Featured's free tier is genuinely limited — real reach needs the paid tier — but even then, you're building a public expert profile across many publications, not just accumulating links. That's worth something on its own if visibility, not just SEO, is the goal, especially for founders building a personal brand alongside their company's.

## Other Featured.com alternatives

If you want expert-quote requests rather than a direct link trade, these are the sites like Featured.com (and Terkel before it) most teams compare. Pricing and plans change, so check each one before you commit time to it.

- **[Exchange Backlinks](/submit)** — not a quote marketplace: a free, weekly match with B2B sites in your niche, where the backlink is the deal itself. The best fit when the link, not the byline, is what you're after.
- **[HARO](/alternatives/haro)** — the classic journalist-request digest, now owned and run for free by Featured.com itself. Three digests a day across every beat.
- **[Help a B2B Writer](/alternatives/help-a-b2b-writer)** — free source requests from writers covering B2B topics, closer to a B2B site's niche than general press requests.
- **[Qwoted](https://www.qwoted.com/)** — connects reporters with expert sources. There's a free plan with a limited number of pitches, and paid plans for more.
- **[Source of Sources (SOS)](https://sourceofsources.com/)** — a free email digest of journalist requests, started in 2023 by Peter Shankman, the original founder of HARO.
- **[SourceBottle](https://www.sourcebottle.com/)** — free call-outs from journalists, bloggers, and podcasters, with its strongest coverage in Australia and New Zealand.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Featured.com the same as Terkel?

Yes. Terkel rebranded as Featured.com, and the core format carried over: publishers post questions, experts answer, and the best answers get quoted.

### Is Featured.com free?

There's a free tier, but it's limited compared to Premium (~$99/month), which adds AI-assisted response tools and broader visibility into opportunities.

### Does Featured.com now own HARO?

Yes. Featured.com acquired the HARO brand from Cision in April 2025 and relaunched it as a free email digest after users rejected Cision's earlier paid "Connectively" version.

### Is a backlink guaranteed if I answer a question on Featured.com?

No. Inclusion and linking depend entirely on whether the publisher's editorial team selects and credits your answer.
